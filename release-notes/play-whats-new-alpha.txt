• Lists can have their own levels, such as course numbers.
• A variant can have its own counts, rhythm, level and description.
• Richer rhythms: 3&a4, held beats like 3(&)4, pickups, S Q Q.

• Terms of use, Report list, import a list as a copy, and fixes.
