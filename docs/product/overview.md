# What DancePatternMapper does

DancePatternMapper (DPM) maps the figures a dancer or a course learns: what each figure builds
on, its variations, and the dancer's own videos of it. It is for dancers recording and sharing
what they learn in courses, and for teachers turning a course's figure list into something their
students can follow. Android first (in closed testing on Google Play), iOS later, with a website
at dancepatternmapper.com.

It works without an account or a network. Sharing is optional and anonymous.

**List**, **Map** and **Reels**.

## Principles that run through every area

- **The phone is the home of the data.** Lists live on the phone. Nothing goes to the cloud unless
  the user publishes, follows, suggests to, asks to join or reports a list.
- **Heavy work stays on the phone.** Video editing, transcription and suggestions run on the
  device. DPM itself uploads no video.
- **The user decides.** A tool's result (a cut video, a suggestion, a suggested change) changes
  nothing until the user keeps it.
- **Read-only means read-only.** A followed list can't be edited, except for the user's own
  arrangement of the network view.
- **Nine languages, every string.** English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, Bengali,
  Portuguese and German.
- **Dance content is exact.** Real figure names, verified terms, variations as modifiers
  ([glossary](glossary.md)).
