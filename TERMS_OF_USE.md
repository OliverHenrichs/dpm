You are responsible for what you share. Share only what you are allowed to share:

- **People.** Do not name or show anyone in a video without their consent. For a child, you need the consent of a parent or guardian. DPM's _Anonymize_ tool can replace dancers with silhouettes.
- **Rights.** Share only videos, music and texts you made yourself or have the right to share. A link to someone else's video is fine if that video is meant to be watched, for example a public or unlisted YouTube video its owner shared with you.
- **Links.** A link must lead to what it says: a dance video, not something else.

## 4. Reporting content

If you find something in DPM that is illegal or breaks these terms, tell us:

- **In the app:** Lists → the list's "more" button → **Report list**. Choose what is wrong and, if you can, say which pattern or video you mean.
- **By email:** dance-pattern-mapper@pm.me, with the list's share code, what you found, and why you believe it is illegal or against these terms.

You may give an email address if you want a reply; it is optional. Please report only in good faith and as accurately as you can. The publisher is not told who reported their list.

We look at every report promptly and act on it if the content is illegal or breaks these terms. Reports about children's safety are handled first.

## 5. What we may do

If content is illegal or breaks these terms, we may **remove it**, **take the list down** so it cannot be published again under the same code, or **block the installation** that published or suggested it from publishing, suggesting and asking to join. We do so in proportion to what happened, and we take into account whether it was a mistake.

DPM has no user accounts, so we usually have no way to contact a publisher. If you have told us an email address, we will tell you what we did and why. If you think we got it wrong, write to us and we will look at it again. If content suggests a crime that threatens someone's life or safety, we inform the authorities.
