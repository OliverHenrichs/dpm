On your own

Keep track of your own progress.

A course teaches a new figure every week, and by the last week the first ones have faded. Film each figure while it's fresh, add the teacher's tips, and keep a reference you can come back to long after the course ends.

Start with what you learned this week

  1. Start a list for your dance

    Pick a template with a few starter figures, or a blank list. Not sure yet? Open the example: a West Coast Swing group's list after two beginner courses and a festival.

  2. Add each figure as it comes

    Give it a name, film it or paste a video link, and jot down what the teacher said. Tag it with the course or the festival weekend you learned it at.

  3. Look back before the next class

    Find a figure in the list, see where it fits on the map, or flick through your videos as reels.

On your phone, not in the cloud

Your class videos, put to work

Add a clip from your gallery, film a new one, or paste a link that starts at the right second. Then let DPM do the tedious part:

  • Keep just the figure: cut a long recording down in seconds
  • Hide who's dancing: turn the dancers into coloured silhouettes, or cover their faces, so you can share the clip with a clear conscience (clips of up to 30 seconds)
  • Read what the teacher said: tap a line to jump to it in the video, correct it, or copy it to your notes
  • Get a name and description suggested from what was said. You decide whether to use it

All of it runs on the phone, and your videos never leave it. Hiding the dancers and writing out the words take a few minutes in the background. DPM doesn't come with videos: every clip is one you recorded, or a link you added.

Reels: your figures, one video at a time

Before class or a social, flick through what you've learned so far. Swipe up or down to the next figure, and sideways through its videos, variations included.

Reels play the list you have open, or every list on your phone.

One list, three ways to see it

Open a list and the bar at the bottom switches between three views of the same figures. The menu holds everything else: your other lists, and Settings.

  • List Every figure with its type and level. Sort it, filter it, open one for the details.
  • Map What you need to know before each figure: as a timeline, or as a network you arrange by hand.
  • Reels The videos of your figures, one per screen.

Open a figure and you get its counts and rhythm (1 2 3&4 5&6 for a six-count West Coast Swing figure), its notes, its videos and its variations.

Back from a festival weekend

More figures than you can remember? Tag them with the festival, filter for the tag in the weeks after, and you see each new figure together with the ones it builds on.

Filter by name, type, level, counts or tags, on the map or in the list. The matches stay bright and the figures they build on fade back.

Variations stay with their figure

A whip with an inside turn is still a whip. Add "with inside turn" to the basic whip instead of making a new figure, and film it as a second video: that figure danced that way.

Some variations, like a hesitation or a hand change, fit any figure, and can carry demo videos of their own.

Questions

How should I use tags?

One tag per course, such as "Beginners 2", or per festival weekend. A course teaches about one figure a week, so a tag per week would hold a single figure. With a tag per course, one list can keep growing over years of classes.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere, unless you send them. Videos stay on your phone, and a backup file includes them only if you choose.

Does the AI send my videos to a server?

No. Hiding the dancers, writing out what was said and suggesting a name all run on the phone. The models download once (Wi-Fi recommended), and you can delete them in Settings.

How do I keep a backup, or move to a new phone?

Your lists live on your phone, so save a copy as a file now and then, with or without the videos. Open the file in DPM, on the same phone or a new one, to bring your lists back. A damaged file can't harm the lists you already have.
