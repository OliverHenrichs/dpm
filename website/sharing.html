With classmates

Film one week each, and everyone has every figure.

One of you keeps the list; the others follow it on their phones and send in the figures they filmed. It works the same for classmates, a practice group or friends who go to the same festivals.

How it works

  1. One of you keeps the list

    The list owner shares it and gets a code of eight letters and digits, plus a QR code, to pass round. Only they can change the list.

  2. The others follow it

    On the Lists screen, tap Subscribe and type the code or scan it. Each of them gets a copy that grows by itself every time a figure is added.

  3. Take turns filming

    Agree who records which week. Whoever filmed puts the clip online and sends in the figure with its link, and the list owner adds it for everyone.

Send in the figure you filmed

Send the figure from your copy of the list: its name, type and counts, a description and the video link, signed with your name if you like. The list owner can correct it and add it with one tap, or dismiss it.

The list owner switches suggestions on first, and can set a daily limit. A suggestion carries video links, never the files; the next section is how a clip on your phone becomes a link.

From your phone to a link

Your classmates can't reach a video that's only on your phone. Open it in the figure and tap Put online: DPM hands it to your YouTube or Vimeo app, where you upload it as unlisted, so only people with the link can find it. Share the link back to DPM, and it takes the file's place. The transcript stays with it.

  • Cut it to the figure first, and hide the dancers if not everyone in it wants to be recognisable
  • Found a good video in the YouTube app? Share it to DPM and add it to a figure
  • YouTube and Vimeo videos, Dropbox links, video files on the web and streams play inside DPM. A link that won't play, such as a Google Drive page, gets a warning

DPM uploads nothing itself. Your own apps do the upload, so the video sits in your account, where you can take it down.

Questions

Who can see our list?

Anyone who has its code, and with it every video link in it. If that's too open, the list owner can make the list members only: then the code lets people ask to join, and the list owner lets in the ones they know. More on that under As a teacher.

Can I add my own videos to the list I follow?

Not directly, since only the list owner changes it. Suggest the figure with your video's link, or keep your own list alongside for your private clips. Reels can play every list on your phone together.

Do my classmates need an account?

No. Following a list needs only its code. Suggesting a figure or asking to join uses a random code instead of a name; nobody signs up.

What if the list owner leaves the class?

They can hand the list over to someone who follows it. The code stays the same, so everyone keeps getting updates. If they simply stop sharing, everyone keeps the copy they have.
