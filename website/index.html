Dance Pattern Mapper

Keep what you learn in class.

DPM is a pattern library for partner dancers and the people who teach them. Collect the figures from your course with your own videos, see how they connect, and share the list with your classmates or your students.

See how your figures connect

Some figures are foundations of the dance. In West Coast Swing, the sugar push, the left side pass, the underarm turn and the whip each stand on their own. Others grow out of them: the sugar tuck out of the sugar push, the tuck turn out of the left side pass, the basket whip out of the basic whip. You record which figure builds on which, and DPM draws the map.

  1. Start a list for your dance

    Pick a template for West Coast Swing, Salsa, Bachata, Brazilian Zouk, Argentine Tango or Lindy Hop, or start from a blank list for any other partner dance.

  2. Add each figure as it comes

    Name, type, counts and rhythm, level, notes, your videos from class, tags such as the course or festival, and the figures it builds on.

  3. Look it up, map it, watch it

    The list for looking things up, the map for how it all connects, and reels for watching your videos one after another.

And the details

One list per group or dance

Each list has its own pattern types, colours and modifiers. The six dance templates come with starter patterns.

Works offline

Everything is stored on your phone. Cloud sharing is optional and only happens when you publish.

No account

You never sign up or sign in. Sharing works with a code, not a profile.

Nine languages

English, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, Français, العربية, বাংলা, Português and Deutsch. DPM follows your phone's language by default.

Two styles, light and dark

After Hours is warm and roomy; Clipboard is dense enough to keep a whole course on one screen. Each in light and dark, with contrast that meets accessibility minimums. This site has both: switch at the top.

Open source

MIT-licensed and developed in the open on GitHub. Issues and ideas are welcome.

Questions

Which dances does it work for?

Any partner dance. There are templates for West Coast Swing, Salsa, Bachata, Brazilian Zouk, Argentine Tango and Lindy Hop, and a blank list lets you set up your own pattern types for anything else.

Do I need an account?

No. You never create an account or sign in. Your lists live on your phone. When you publish a list, suggest a pattern to one or ask to join one, the app quietly gets an anonymous ID, so that only you can change your list and a publisher can let a subscriber in, or mute one, without knowing who they are.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere, unless you send them. Videos stay on your device. An export can include them if you choose, and a published list only shares video links, never the files.

Does the AI send my videos to a server?

No. Anonymizing, transcribing and suggesting all run on the phone. The models download once (Wi-Fi recommended), and you can delete them in Settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. DPM comes to Android first, and iOS follows.
