See how your figures connect

Some figures are foundations of the dance. In West Coast Swing, the sugar push, the left side pass, the underarm turn and the whip each stand on their own. Others grow out of them: the sugar tuck out of the sugar push, the travelling tuck out of the left side pass, the basket whip out of the basic whip.

For each figure, note what you need to know first, and DPM draws the map. Before a new figure, you see at a glance which ones to brush up on.

How it works

  1. Start a list for your dance

    Pick a template for West Coast Swing, Salsa, Bachata, Brazilian Zouk, Argentine Tango or Lindy Hop, or start from a blank list for any other partner dance.

  2. Add each figure as it comes

    Give it a name, film it or paste a video link, and jot down what the teacher said. Tag it with the course.

  3. Look it up, map it, watch it

    Find a figure in the list, see how it all connects on the map, and watch your videos one after another before class.

On your phone, not in the cloud

Your class videos, put to work

Cut a long recording down to the figure, turn the dancers into silhouettes before you share it, and get the teacher's words as text you can read back. It all happens on your phone; your videos never leave it.

Good to know

Works offline

Everything is stored on your phone, so it works in a basement studio too. Nothing goes online unless you share a list.

No sign-up

No account, no password, no email address. Sharing works with a code, not a profile.

Your videos stay yours

Videos stay on your phone. A shared list carries only links to videos you put online yourself.

Any partner dance

Templates with starter figures for six dances, or a blank list for any other. One list per group or dance.

Nine languages

English, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, Français, العربية, বাংলা, Português and Deutsch. DPM follows your phone's language by default.

Open source

Questions

Which dances does it work for?

Any partner dance. There are templates for West Coast Swing, Salsa, Bachata, Brazilian Zouk, Argentine Tango and Lindy Hop, and a blank list lets you set up your own figure types for anything else.

Do I need an account?

No. You never sign up or sign in, and nobody asks for your email. When you share a list, suggest a figure or ask to join one, the app uses a random code instead of your name.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere, unless you send them. Videos stay on your phone. A shared list carries only video links, never the files.

Does the AI send my videos to a server?

No. Hiding the dancers, writing out what was said and suggesting a name all run on the phone. The models download once (Wi-Fi recommended), and you can delete them in Settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. DPM comes to Android first, and iOS follows.
