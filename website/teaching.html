As a teacher

Your whole course on your students' phones.

Add each figure as you teach it, with your demo video. Your students have it the same evening, and can watch it before the next class. You decide who gets in.

Setting up a course

  1. One list for the group

    Start from a template or a blank list, with your own figure types and variations. Add each figure as you teach it, with a tag for the course.

  2. Share it and show the code

    Sharing gives the list a code of eight letters and digits, plus a QR code to show in class. Students who scan it or type the code get a copy that updates by itself.

  3. Let it grow

    Every figure you add appears on their phones, and the list carries on when the group moves up to the next course.

A list that grows with your group

Groups often stay together for years. Rather than a new list each term, keep one, and tag each figure with the course it belongs to, or the festival weekend you brought it back from.

Your students filter for this course's tag and see each new figure together with the ones they need to know first, faded behind it. Variations stay with their figure, such as the basic whip "with inside turn", so the list doesn't fill up with near-duplicates.

Decide who gets in

A shared list is as private as its code. Members only closes it, so the code alone no longer opens it.

Open

Anyone with the code

The default. Good for a festival class or an open practice group, where you'd hand the code to anyone who asks.

Members only

Only the people you let in

  • Students who enter the code ask to join, with a name
  • You let in the ones you know, or decline
  • Someone you remove keeps the copy they have, but gets no further updates
  • Best chosen when you first share the list
Members only + password

A password on the videos too

Vimeo videos can ask for a password, which you tell your group in class. Members see the list; only someone with the password plays the video.

Switching to members only later shuts out everyone who follows the list until they ask to join and you let them in. Anyone you let in can still pass a video link on, so share with people you trust.

Decide how much your class adds

Let your students suggest figures for your list: its name, type and counts, a description and video links. A suggestion waits in your list until you correct it and add it with one tap, or dismiss it.

  • Off until you switch it on, in the list's sharing settings
  • A daily limit, per student or for the whole list
  • Pause anyone who sends too many, and let them back later
  • On a members-only list, only members can suggest

Handy when students take turns filming the figure of the week: they send it in, you check it before the whole group sees it.

Where your videos live

A shared list carries video links, not files, so your clips live on a video site. These play right inside DPM, so your students never need to leave the app:

YouTube, unlisted

Free, and hidden from search: only someone with the link finds it. Add the link with the second the figure starts.

Vimeo, private link or password

An unlisted Vimeo video plays from its private link, which DPM keeps intact. A password-protected one asks for the password in the player.

Dropbox and other links

Dropbox share links play too. A link that won't play, such as a Google Drive page, gets a warning before you add it.

Straight from your phone

Filmed it in class? Put online hands the clip to your YouTube or Vimeo app and swaps it for the link when you share that back. Cut it to the figure and hide the dancers first, all on the phone, in a few minutes.

Questions

Can my students change my list?

No. Their copies are read-only and follow every change you make. Only you can update the list or stop sharing it.

What happens if I stop sharing?

The cloud copy is deleted right away. Your students keep their copy, and it becomes theirs to edit, without further updates.

Can I hand the list over to another teacher?

Yes. Hand it over to someone who follows it, and they become the list owner. The code stays the same, so your students keep getting updates.

Which video links play in DPM?

YouTube and Vimeo, Dropbox share links, direct links to a video file on your own web space, and HLS or DASH streams.

Do I see who my students are?

Only the name they give when they ask to join or suggest a figure. Behind it is a random code, with no account or contact details.
